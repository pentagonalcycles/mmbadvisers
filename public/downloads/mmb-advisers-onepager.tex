\section{THE PROBLEM}

{\footnotesize Upside participation remains attractive, but valuations are stretched, leadership is concentrated in a handful of mega-caps, and geopolitical shocks arrive with little warning.\enspace Most risk tools are reactive: they confirm the damage after it has happened.\enspace And losses compound:

\begin{center}
\colorbox{lightgrey}{\parbox{0.88\linewidth}{\centering\sffamily\footnotesize
  a \textbf{$-$20\%} drawdown needs \textbf{+25\%} to recover;\quad
  a \textbf{$-$50\%} drawdown needs \textbf{+100\%}.
}}
\end{center}
}

% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
%  THE SOLUTION
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
\section{THE SOLUTION}

{\footnotesize Each year MMB publishes, in advance, a calendar of \textbf{12 candidate turning-point dates} grouped into two or three high-risk windows.\enspace The calendar is set at the end of the prior year and \textbf{not revised} ($\pm$1 day).\enspace Four proprietary indicators (built on behavioural greed--fear cycles and machine learning) cross-check each window; conviction is highest where they agree.}

\begin{itemize}
  \item{\footnotesize Hedge overlay via liquid futures and options, \textbf{up to three times a year}}
  \item{\footnotesize No liquidation of core long-term holdings (unless specifically preferred by the PM)}
\end{itemize}
